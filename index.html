Jabari Jacobs — Washington DC Commercial & Editorial Photographer

Jabari Jacobs is a commercial and editorial photographer and director based in Washington DC, Los Angeles, and New York. With over ten years of experience, Jabari has served as staff photographer for the U.S. Senate and built a client roster that includes Netflix, Disney, Nike, Adidas, ESPN, Atlantic Records, Condé Nast, The Washington Post, and more. Available worldwide for advertising, editorial, music, and food photography projects.
